# Mechanical Systems

These notes apply kinematics, dynamics, strength of materials and fluid mechanics to the components that make up machines: the linkages and cams that shape motion, the gears that change speed and torque, the bearings that carry rotating shafts, the rotors and turbomachines that exchange energy with a flow, and the fluid power circuits that transmit force through pressurized oil and air. Each note poses the analysis problem, derives the working equations, and checks them with a script where a computed figure helps: four-bar kinematics by loop closure, involute gear geometry, a Reynolds-equation solver for journal bearings, the Jeffcott rotor and Campbell diagram, and pump and system curves.

## Table of Contents

### 1. [Mechanisms and Linkages](./mechanisms.md)

Kinematic pairs and mobility (Grübler–Kutzbach), the Grashof condition and four-bar inversions, position analysis with the Freudenstein equation, velocity and acceleration by loop closure, the transmission angle and toggle positions, slider-crank kinematics with primary and secondary inertia terms, cam follower motions and pressure angle, and three-point function synthesis.

### 2. [Gears and Transmissions](./gears.md)

The fundamental law of gearing, the involute profile, module and tooth proportions, contact ratio, interference and the minimum number of teeth, helical, bevel and worm gears, simple, compound and planetary trains with the Willis equation, tooth forces, the Lewis bending stress and efficiency.

### 3. [Bearings and Lubrication](./bearings.md)

Rolling-bearing types, equivalent load and $L_{10}$ rating life with reliability adjustment; the Reynolds equation derived from Navier–Stokes for thin films, Petroff's law, long- and short-bearing solutions, cavitation, the Sommerfeld number and Raimondi–Boyd design data; the Stribeck curve and elastohydrodynamic films.

### 4. [Rotating Machinery](./rotating_machinery.md)

The Jeffcott rotor, critical speeds and the phase change through resonance, gyroscopic effects and the Campbell diagram, single- and two-plane balancing with influence coefficients, flywheels, the Euler turbomachine equation and velocity triangles, specific speed, pump and system curves, affinity laws and NPSH.

### 5. [Hydraulic and Pneumatic Systems](./hydraulic_pneumatic.md)

Pascal's law and force multiplication, cylinders and motors, positive-displacement pumps, valves and the orifice equation, line losses, accumulators, hydraulic power and heat, compression of air, the stiffness of oil and air columns and why pneumatic positioning is hard, and circuit symbols.
