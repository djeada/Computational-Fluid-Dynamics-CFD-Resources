# Strength of Materials

Strength of materials predicts the stresses, strains and deformations of bars, shafts, beams and columns from their geometry, supports, loads and material properties, and checks them against strength, stiffness and stability limits. These notes start from stress and strain at a point and the measured properties of materials, treat the basic member types one loading at a time (axial force, torsion, shear and bending, buckling), combine them, and end with failure under cyclic loading. The fluid loads that drive many of these members are in the [fluid loading](../fluid_loading/intro.md) notes.
