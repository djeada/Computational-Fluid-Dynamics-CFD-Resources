# Cars

These notes treat the passenger car as a mechanical system and as a product. The first explains where the energy goes and how the powertrain, transmission, brakes, tyres and suspension work, using the road-load equation, the Otto cycle, gearing relations, tyre friction and the quarter-car model; the second follows a car from targets and packaging through the aerodynamic development loop of CFD and wind tunnel, crash structures, and production in the press, body, paint and assembly shops. Together they give the vehicle context for the [automotive aerodynamics case study](../../../machine_learning/automotive_aerodynamics/README.md).

## Table of Contents

### 1. [How Cars Work](./how_cars_work.md)

The road-load equation and crossover speed, power and energy demand, combustion engines and the Otto efficiency, electric motors and batteries, gear ratios, braking, load transfer and tyre slip, the quarter-car ride model, and the drag area, lift and cooling drag of a car.

### 2. [Car Design and Manufacturing](./manufacturing_and_design_process.md)

The vehicle development process and target cascade, packaging, the aerodynamic development loop with CFD, wind tunnels and coastdown tests, crash energy absorption, and stamping, joining, painting and final assembly of the body.
