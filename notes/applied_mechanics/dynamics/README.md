# Dynamics

Dynamics relates the motion of particles and rigid bodies to the forces and moments that cause it. These notes start with the description of motion in Cartesian, path and polar coordinates, apply Newton's second law to particles and rigid bodies, integrate it into the work–energy and impulse–momentum principles, and end with vibration and with Lagrange's equations, which give the equations of motion of constrained systems and the mass and stiffness matrices used in structural and multibody analysis.

## Table of Contents

### 1. [Kinematics of Particles](./kinematics_particles.md)

Rectilinear motion with constant and variable acceleration, projectiles, normal–tangential and polar coordinates with the radius of curvature and the Coriolis term, and relative motion between translating frames.

### 2. [Kinetics of Particles](./kinetics_particles.md)

Newton's second law in Cartesian, path and polar coordinates, force models, central-force motion and orbits, projectiles with quadratic drag, and the motion of the mass centre of a system of particles.

### 3. [Rigid Body Kinematics](./rigid_body_kinematics.md)

Translation, rotation about a fixed axis, general plane motion, the instantaneous centre, relative velocity and acceleration with $\omega$ and $\alpha$, the slider-crank, and rotating reference frames with the Coriolis acceleration.

### 4. [Rigid Body Kinetics](./rigid_body_kinetics.md)

Mass moments of inertia and the parallel-axis theorem, the equations of plane motion, rolling with and without slip, and Euler's equations with the stability of spin about principal axes and gyroscopic moments.

### 5. [Work and Energy Methods](./work_energy.md)

Work of forces and couples, kinetic energy of rigid bodies, potential energy and conservation of energy, power and efficiency, and the energy balance as a check on numerical integration.

### 6. [Impulse and Momentum](./impulse_momentum.md)

Linear and angular impulse and momentum, conservation laws, central and oblique impact with the coefficient of restitution, steady mass flow, and variable-mass systems with the rocket equation.

### 7. [Mechanical Vibrations](./vibrations.md)

Free and damped single-degree-of-freedom vibration, the logarithmic decrement, harmonic forcing with magnification and phase, base excitation and isolation, and multi-degree-of-freedom modes from $\mathbf{K}\boldsymbol{\phi} = \omega^2\mathbf{M}\boldsymbol{\phi}$ with the tuned mass damper.

### 8. [Lagrangian Mechanics](./lagrangian_mechanics.md)

Generalized coordinates and forces, Lagrange's equations from d'Alembert's principle, Rayleigh's dissipation function, the cart–pendulum and double pendulum, and linearization about equilibrium to obtain $\mathbf{M}$, $\mathbf{C}$ and $\mathbf{K}$.
